# 14. Recommended Next Actions

> A prioritized, concrete sequence from concept to prototype. Numbers are order, not dates; items 1–5 can start immediately and cost little.

1. **Assemble the paper decision corpus (the cheapest thesis test).** Before any code: collect ~50 real translation decisions from one or two willing teams (mine their Paratext project notes and Biblical Terms comments, plus interviews). Hand-model them in the [§4](04-domain-model.md) schema on paper/spreadsheet. Count the phenomena the thesis predicts: dependency chains, precedent-reuse events, recurring anchors. **If they aren't there, stop and revise the thesis** ([§11 Phase 0 exit](11-phased-roadmap.md)).
2. **Recruit 1–2 pilot partners and a consultant advisor.** Warm paths: SIL/UBS innovation contacts, ETEN Innovation Lab, unfoldingWord community. Bring [§15](15-worked-example.md) (the worked example) and the [§10.9](10-mvp-definition.md) demo script as the conversation piece; bring [§13.5](13-risks-and-open-questions.md) (governance) to show the data-sovereignty homework is done.
3. **Terminology walkthrough with practitioners.** Test the [§2](02-terminology-and-conceptual-model.md) vocabulary (Issue/Decision/treatment/anchor) with 5–8 translators and consultants; revise where it fights their mental model. Cheap now, expensive after it's in the UI.
4. **Pin the data foundations.** Download and version-pin MACULA (Greek+Hebrew), SDBH/SDGNT extracts, Copenhagen versification JSON, Glottolog/Grambank CLDF slices; stand up the resource registry with per-attribute licensing notes ([§3.2](03-research-and-prior-art-landscape.md)); assemble the Jonah reference dataset ([§10.4](10-mvp-definition.md)). Resolve the Biblical Terms list licensing question with UBS/SIL contacts (fallback: hand-built Jonah term list).
5. **Verify the handful of [unverified] flags** in [§3](03-research-and-prior-art-landscape.md) that affect near-term choices (Paratext notes XML schema; Biblical Terms license; Platform.Bible data-access terms) — direct emails to the respective teams beat web archaeology.
6. **Write the JSON Schemas + SKOS vocabularies (Phase 1).** Categories, treatments, rationale types, dependency types, event catalog. Round-trip the paper corpus through them; fix what doesn't fit.
7. **Build the deterministic core (Phase 2).** Event log → projections → dependency rules → graph metrics → anchor-intersection retrieval, headless, test-driven, on the Jonah dataset. No UI, no AI. This is ~the smallest artifact that makes the thesis executable.
8. **Run the retrieval bench pilot early.** Even with 50–80 seeded records: have two consultants judge top-5 candidates for 20 query issues. A weak signal here (vs. the fuzzy-text baseline, [§12.3](12-efficacy-study.md)) is the cheapest possible warning that anchors need embeddings support or denser tagging.
9. **Build the MVP UI (Phase 3)** — the seven screens of [§10.7](10-mvp-definition.md), plus the two minimal AI assists behind the audited gateway.
10. **Two-week live trial with the pilot team**, instrumented ([§12.4](12-efficacy-study.md) Track B/E logging from day one); weekly iteration; SUS + trust instruments at the close.
11. **Run the scripted demonstration ([§10.9](10-mvp-definition.md)) for professional translators** at a partner venue (e.g., a consultant gathering or ETEN-adjacent event); collect structured reactions; recruit wave-2 pilots.
12. **Then decide** — with pre-registered criteria ([§12.7](12-efficacy-study.md)) — whether the evidence supports Phase 4 (full pilot + study), a pivot (e.g., consultant-checking-first positioning), or an honest stop.

Deferred by design (do not start yet): cross-project sharing features (Phase-6 gate), the discourse SKOS scheme (needs SIL co-ownership, Phase 5), Platform.Bible packaging (churn risk until their 1.0), any model fine-tuning.
